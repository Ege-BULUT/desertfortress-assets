# Desert Fortress: site assets

Public assets streamed by the Desert Fortress website (desertfortress.vercel.app), mainly its /lore page,
so the site deployment itself stays small.

## What may go in here
- Assets made for, or shipped in, the Desert Fortress web prototype (models, textures, audio) that we are
  free to publish.
- Renders, images and videos made from the Unreal Engine project.

## What must never go in here
- Raw files of third-party assets whose licence forbids redistribution, such as Fab or Quixel Megascans
  meshes, textures or materials (GLB, FBX, uasset, source textures). Only renders and videos made with them
  may be published.

Every folder lists its sources and licences in LICENSES.md.
