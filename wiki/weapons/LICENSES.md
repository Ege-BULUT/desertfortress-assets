# Licences: wiki/weapons

Everything in this folder was made from the Desert Fortress Unreal Engine project on 2026-11-25. **41 of 41 guns may be shown as interactive 3D (GLB)**: every gun model in the game is CC0 or CC-BY 4.0 and textured with its own CC-BY textures or our CC0 material library. No Fab or Megascans file is in this folder. Attachments are pictures only (their in-game models come from a Fab pack).

## What is in here and under which terms

- `cards/`, `attachments/`, `contact_sheet.jpg`: renders made in Unreal Engine 5.8 (studio lighting, engine shapes only). Renders of the models below; credit the CC-BY authors where the gun is shown.
- `models/*.glb`: web versions of the in-game gun meshes (<= 30k triangles, textures <= 2048 px, JPEG). Same licence as the source model; CC-BY needs the credit line below wherever the model is shown.
- `stats.json`, `STATS.md`, `licences.json`: our own data, free to use on the site.
